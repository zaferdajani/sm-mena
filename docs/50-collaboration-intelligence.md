# 50 · Collaboration V2, release 3: planner, worksheet and templates, collaborator feedback, next actions

Release 3 adds what makes repeat work easier once agencies collaborate through inquiries (docs/48) and work orders (docs/49). Nothing here commits anyone or moves money: a plan books nobody, a template starts a draft, feedback never changes a rating, a reminder only points at something the agency can already open. Feature switch `collaboration_intelligence` (docs/34).

## Scope-to-team planner

Studio → Collaborate → Plan. The agency gives a title, deliverables from the contract catalogue and a brief. `lib/collab/planner.ts` (pure) groups the deliverables into packages by catalogue group and maps each deliverable to the roles that usually deliver it (`DELIVERABLE_ROLES`, taxonomy only). Coverage per role is decided from the agency's own facts outward: `in_house` when the agency declared the role, `partner` when an accepted partner declared it, `candidate` when someone on the roster or an authorized discovery read (`discoverCollaborators`, the same read the Discover page makes) matched it, else `unfilled`. Every plan stores `sources`: which read produced which ids, shown on the plan page. A candidate is a suggestion with a link to inquire; the planner never writes partner requests, holds or matching scores (unit test asserts no rows change).

**Optional assistant.** `lib/ai/planner.ts` may regroup and retitle the packages, nothing else. It receives only the redacted brief plus the catalogue keys; it has no tools and a 20 s timeout. Redaction (`lib/collab/redact.ts`) strips invisible/bidi characters and tashkeel, normalises Arabic-Indic digits, then removes private sections, credential-like strings, URLs, emails (including "[at]"/"[dot]" forms; a literal dot never spans lines), phone-like runs of nine or more digits (ISO dates survive) and @handles. Private markers are "private", "private note(s)", "internal", "confidential", "do not send", "not for the supplier", "خاص", "سري", "داخلي", "لا يُرسل", "ملاحظة خاصة" and "للداخل"; the Arabic words may carry the clitic prefixes of written Arabic (an optional و/ف, then an optional ب/ك/ل and/or ال, with ل+ال as لل: الخاص, بالخاص, للخاص, وبالخاص …), and every marker must start at a word boundary so "privately:" or a word that merely ends in a marker is not one. Markdown decoration (`**`, `_`, quotes) may surround the word and follow the colon. The forms are handled by a small scanner, not a single regex: a `[private]…[/private]` or `<private>…</private>` span is removed exactly and spans nest (an inner close never exposes the outer span's tail; an unclosed span runs to the end of the text); a bracketed "(private: …)" note is removed to the matching close of the same bracket kind, other brackets inside do not end it, and an unclosed one runs to the end of its paragraph; a marker that starts a line, or one after which the rest of the line is empty apart from decoration, opens a section removed up to the next blank line, an "end:/public:/عام:/نهاية:" line or the end of the text; a marker inside a line with text after it removes the rest of that line. CRLF is normalised first. The rule errs toward removal; the plan page shows the redacted brief so the agency sees what was used. Redaction is the second line: the request is built by `plannerRequest` from a `PlannerBrief` that has exactly three fields (title, scope, deliverables) and throws on any other key, so the plan's **private notes** column (`collab_plans.private_notes`, entered in the form, shown only to the agency), roster notes, rates, partner or client names and the agency's own record cannot reach a model by construction; `tests/unit/planner-hardening.test.ts` plants synthetic secrets in every field and asserts the captured request carries none of them. Its output is accepted only if it uses exactly the brief's deliverable keys once each (`applyModelPackages`); roles are re-derived from the taxonomy and titles are redacted, so neither roles nor candidate ids can come from the model. Budget: 20 assistant requests per agency per UTC day, reserved atomically **before** the call with one upsert on `collab_ai_usage` (`lib/data/collab-ai-usage.ts`; primary key agency + day, `used < budget` as the update condition). The same row serves every serverless instance, a refused reservation makes no call, a made call keeps its reservation whether it succeeds, fails, times out or returns junk, and deleting plans refunds nothing (the old count over `collab_plans.assistant_requested` is gone; that column is informational). The Postgres rehearsal races 50 reservations from two connection pools and exactly 20 win. Rows are one per agency and assistant day; the daily cron deletes rows older than 400 days (`purgeAssistantUsage`), which cannot affect a current budget. No key, `AI_PROVIDER=mock|basic|off`, timeout, bad JSON, invalid keys, an exhausted budget or a planted test marker in the request all leave the rule-based draft in place, and the plan page says which happened. The stored brief is the redacted text. "Edit as a new plan" prefills the form with a previous plan's brief; the rules run again on what the agency changes (packages themselves are not edited in place).

## Cost worksheet and templates

Studio → Collaborate → Plan → Cost worksheet (`lib/data/collab-worksheet.ts`): the buyer's work orders and accepted quotes with supplier, quoted amount, due date and status, grouped by the buyer's own client contract (the private parent). Per group: supplier costs per currency (declined, withdrawn and cancelled engagements are listed but not counted), the number of lines without an amount, and a margin against the client contract only when every known cost is in the contract's currency; mixed currencies show a note instead of a sum. The page says these are estimates from accepted quotes, not quotations or commitments. One login per agency, so the owner is the only finance viewer; suppliers see an empty sheet of their own.

Templates (`lib/collab/templates.ts`): shoot, reels, Arabic copy, ad creative, monthly calendar. Each carries deliverables, a scope skeleton and review rules only. `work/new?template=<key>` starts an inquiry draft from one. **Rehire**: `work/new?rehire=<workOrderId>` (buyer only) copies the accepted version's deliverables and scope and preselects the supplier; dates, budget, compensation reference, permission scope, files and access are not copied (`rehireDraft`, tested). Every cycle is a new inquiry the supplier answers; there is no automatic renewal, charge or hold.

## Collaborator feedback

`collab_feedback`: one record per work order and side, written only by the two parties once the order is approved or closed; a retry returns the existing record (unique index, `onConflictDoNothing`). Three scores (communication, reliability, quality), a note, and the author's publication choice: `parties` (the two of them) or `public`. The public page of the subject shows public, published records under its own heading "Collaborator feedback" with the author's name and role only: never the work order, the client or files. The subject can opt out of public display (`collab_prefs.show_feedback`) and can dispute a record, which hides it from public view until staff decide in Admin → Reviews (publish or hide, audited; text is never edited). Public display is also off whenever the `collaboration_intelligence` switch is not globally on. Client reviews, Google ratings and paid-project provenance (`lib/reviews/provenance.ts`) are untouched and never averaged with this class.

## Next actions, reminders and preferences

`lib/data/collab-next.ts` computes what waits on an agency from the same rows the pages show: offers to answer, submissions to review, amendments to answer, inquiries to reply to, quotes to compare, holds expiring within two days, stale availability, feedback to leave. The Discover page shows them oldest-due first. The daily cron (`/api/cron/retention`, `maxDuration` 60 s) runs only while the switch is globally on, over at most 300 agencies with something open (ordered ids), and writes at most one in-app reminder per open item and day (`reminder_*` notification kinds, deduplicated against the delivery time so a reminder deferred by quiet hours still counts for its own day only), skips muted kinds, skips demo agencies, and delivers reminders due inside the agency's quiet hours at the end of them (scheduled `created_at`, the existing mechanism). Reminders carry a kind, a title and a link only. Preferences: Studio → Collaborate → Discover → Reminders (`collab_prefs`, plus the feedback opt-out).

## Metrics

Admin → Statistics → "Collaboration funnel": `lib/data/collab-metrics.ts` derives the steps from the records themselves (inquiries, recipients, quotes, work orders, submissions, milestones, ledger releases), so retries cannot inflate a step. Demo agencies and agencies owned by staff are excluded on both sides (buyer and supplier) and counted as such. Payout initiated (a non-failed ledger release) and direct payment confirmed are separate steps. The 30-day repeat rate is reported only for buyer–supplier pairs whose first work order is at least 30 days old, with numerator and denominator; the median approval-to-payout delay is given with its n; direct confirmations have no timestamp of their own, so their delay is not measured. No agency is identified.
