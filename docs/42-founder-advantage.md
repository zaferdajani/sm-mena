# 42 · Founder Advantage program

Owner-approved program for qualifying providers who join before the founding cohort closes.

## Why it exists

Founder status is not a collectible number and never overrides relevance. It rewards providers who help Sawwiq build useful supply before buyers arrive.

## Approved benefits

1. **First Sawwiq-acquired completed project: 0% Sawwiq platform commission.**
   - Applies only after protected payments are live.
   - Payment-provider charges, taxes or pass-through charges are separate and must be disclosed.
2. **Founder platform rate: 7% for the first year of live protected payments** after the first-project waiver.
3. **Qualified opportunity head start:** target 24-hour early window during the launch period, only for opportunities the provider is already relevant for. It never changes relevance scoring.
4. **Six months of Pro tools**, starting when Pro actually launches, not when the provider registered.
5. **Existing-client workflow founder terms:** founders may bring their own clients into Sawwiq contracts/workflow under a lower infrastructure fee to be finalized after the payment partner's real costs are known. Do not publish a percentage until approved.
6. **Partner-network opportunities:** founders receive early access to relevant agency-to-agency/freelancer subcontracting opportunities during launch.
7. **Permanent dated Founder status (2026)** and early product access/feedback. It is not verification, a ranking, ownership or a guarantee of work.

## Eligibility

A registration alone is not enough for commercial founder benefits. Before benefits activate, the provider must:
- be a real, non-demo active provider;
- join before the founding cutoff/cohort capacity;
- complete the core profile (bio + approved services);
- publish genuine work or a useful package before the activation cutoff;
- satisfy identity/KYC requirements that apply when money features go live.

The existing founding seat remains an internal immutable join-order record. It is not marketed as the prize.

## Trust rules

- Never say "guaranteed leads", "guaranteed clients", "best agency", or imply Founder means quality.
- Early opportunity access is relevance-gated; later providers can rank above founders.
- The 0% waiver and 7% rate refer to Sawwiq's platform commission only.
- No real-money claim goes live before a licensed payment partner and legal review.
- Existing-client fee is deliberately TBD until partner costs are known.
