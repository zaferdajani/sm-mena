# 07 · Go-to-Market Playbook

## 1. Sequence

Supply → demand → monetisation → transaction. Never open demand before there are 30 verified agencies to route to. Never charge before agencies have won clients for free.

## 2. Supply: the first 40 agencies (Months 1–3)

**Seed.** Build a spreadsheet of every agency and studio in Jordan from Clutch, DesignRush, Sortlist, TechBehemoths, Entasher, Instagram hashtag searches (#وكالة_تسويق_الأردن, #تسويق_الكتروني_عمان), and LinkedIn. Target 120 rows. Use `data/agency-seed-template.csv`. Import them as `seeded` profiles.

**Rank.** Tier A: 25 agencies with strong portfolios and 5+ staff. Tier B: 40 boutique studios. Tier C: the rest.

**Outreach, Tier A first.** Founder calls or visits. Script:
> "We are building the place Jordanian businesses go to find a verified agency. Your profile is already up. I want you as a founding member: lifetime free Pro, featured at launch, and a say in how verification works. All I need is 20 minutes to verify you and your packages."

**Founding member offer (first 40, deadline Month 3):**
- Lifetime free Pro (worth 588 JOD/year)
- Homepage feature for the first 6 months
- Founding badge on profile, permanently
- Advisory WhatsApp group with the founder
- In return: complete profile with packages and prices, verification documents, two references, 48h response commitment, and a "Get a quote via Sawwiq" link in their Instagram bio for 6 months

**Verification standard (publish it):**
1. Companies Control Department registration certificate matching the trading name
2. Live portfolio with at least 3 real clients
3. Two client references contacted by Sawwiq
4. Named account owner with work email
5. Response-time commitment (48h) accepted

## 3. Demand: the first 100 briefs (Months 3–6)

**Paid social.** Meta + Snapchat, Arabic creative, targeting page admins and business-interest audiences in Jordan. Budget 1,200 JOD/month. Creative angles:
- "كم يجب أن تدفع لإدارة السوشيال ميديا في الأردن؟" (What should you pay for social media management in Jordan?)
- "٣ عروض من وكالات موثّقة خلال ٤٨ ساعة، مجاناً"
- Before/after case study from a founding agency

**Organic search.** Nothing exists in Arabic. Publish: pricing guide, "how to choose an agency", 10 city pages, 12 service pages, 8 industry pages. Each page has the brief form embedded.

**Partnerships (Year 1 targets):**
- JEDCO: become a listed vendor channel for "Raise your capabilities" marketing grant recipients
- Amman Chamber of Commerce / Chamber of Industry: member benefit
- Coworking spaces and incubators: iPark, Oasis500 portfolio, Zain Innovation Campus
- Bank SME arms (Capital Bank, Bank al Etihad, Arab Bank SME): co-branded "find a marketing partner" page
- Orange/Zain/Umniah business units: bundle in SME packages (Year 2)

**Supply markets demand.** Every verified agency gets a widget/link. When their own site visitors click "Get a quote", the brief is routed to that agency plus two others. The agency gets its own lead free; the other two pay. This turns agencies into an acquisition channel.

**Agency Day, Month 4.** Half-day event in Amman. 15 founding agencies present 5-minute case studies. 100 invited business owners. Brief kiosk at the door. Goal: 40 briefs in one day and press coverage in Jordan Times/Al Ghad/Roya.

## 4. Monetisation switch (Month 7)

Announce 60 days ahead. Show each agency its own numbers: briefs received, proposals sent, wins. Offer Pro at 49 JOD/month with first month free. Lead fee 15 JOD starts the same day. Founding members exempt from both.

## 5. Metrics that matter

| Metric | Phase 1 target | Phase 2 target |
|---|---|---|
| Verified agencies | 40 | 70 |
| Briefs / month | 30 | 60 |
| Qualified rate | 60% | 65% |
| Briefs with ≥3 proposals within 48h | 70% | 85% |
| Hire rate (qualified) | 25% | 35% |
| Paying agencies | 0 | 15 |
| Buyer NPS | measure | ≥ 40 |
| Agency NPS | measure | ≥ 30 |

## 6. Brand

Arabic name, Arabic voice, Jordanian register. Trustworthy, not flashy. Green/white palette avoids looking like a bank or a telco. Tagline candidate: "وكالتك الصح، بثلاث خطوات" (The right agency, in three steps).
