**What this means.** The average first contract is worth roughly 600–1,800 JOD/month to the agency. A lead that converts is worth several thousand JOD in lifetime value. Agencies will pay 10–25 JOD for a qualified lead once conversion is proven. Global directories already charge them far more.

## 7. Regional expansion context

Jordanian agencies already sell into Saudi Arabia, Iraq and the Gulf (Jeel Media operates in Doha and Istanbul; Sudacé has clients in seven countries). Gulf CPMs are 2–3× Jordan's, so Gulf clients pay more for the same work. The platform can take Jordanian supply to Gulf and Iraqi demand without recruiting new agencies, which is the reverse of Entasher's Egypt-to-Gulf path.
