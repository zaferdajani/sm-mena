# 07 · Decision interviews before spending: 20 buyers, 15 providers (Jordan first)

Purpose (marketing/06 §4): learn how the last real hire happened, what was compared, what caused hesitation, what went wrong and why they renewed or left. Then test three provider messages. Nothing here is a survey; every question is about a specific past event.

Rules: 30–40 minutes, in Arabic (the buyer's dialect), one interviewer and one note-taker, recorded only with consent, no pitching in the first 25 minutes, no leading questions. Ask for a redacted brief or quotation where permission is possible. Pay nothing; offer a summary of what we learn.

## Who

- **Buyers (20, Amman first):** 8 restaurants and cafés, 4 clinics or pharmacies, 3 shops or e-commerce, 3 real estate or professional services, 2 gyms or salons. Half must have hired an agency or freelancer in the last 12 months; the rest must have tried and stopped.
- **Providers (15):** 6 agencies (2–40 people), 6 freelancers (design, photo/video, ads, copy), 3 people who run pages in-house at a business. At least 4 who also *buy* (subcontract photographers, media buyers).
- **Saudi discovery group (later, 8 + 6):** the same scripts with SAR and Saudi examples; not before the Jordan round is read.

## Buyer script («صاحب المشروع»)

1. **The last time.** «آخر مرة وظّفت حدا يسوّق لك أو يدير صفحاتك، كيف صارت؟» Who, when, for what, for how long.
2. **How they found them.** «كيف وصلت إله؟» (a friend, Instagram, a DM, a PDF, a Facebook group, a search). What else did they look at?
3. **What they compared.** «شو قارنت بالضبط؟» Prices, examples of work, who they worked with, speed of reply, the person's manner. Ask to see the quote or price list if they still have it.
4. **The hesitation.** «شو خوّفك قبل ما توقّع أو تدفع؟» Paying up front, not knowing what "12 posts" includes, who owns the account, results.
5. **What went wrong.** «شو صار غلط، لو صار؟» Late, unclear scope, disappearing, weak results, ownership fights.
6. **Renewal.** «ليش كمّلت معهم أو وقّفت؟» What would have kept them.
7. **Price sense.** «كم بتتوقع يكلف شهر إدارة حسابات + تصوير مرة بالشهر؟» Then what they actually paid.
8. **Ownership.** «مين عنده كلمة سر الحسابات والملفات؟»
9. **If a tool existed.** Only now: describe Sawwiq in two sentences (find, compare real work, agree a clear scope). «شو أول إشي بتعمله فيه؟» Watch for: browse, ask the matcher, ask a person on WhatsApp.
10. **Permission.** May we quote you anonymously? May we see the brief/quote redacted?

Record for each: segment, city, last spend per month (JOD), channel that found the provider, top hesitation, top reason to renew or leave, verbatim quotes.

## Provider script («الوكالة / المستقل»)

1. **The last client.** «آخر عميل وصلك، كيف وصلك؟» Referral, Instagram, a job post, a friend of a friend.
2. **Losing work.** «آخر عميل خسرته لمين راح وليش؟» Price, a cousin, ghosting, scope creep.
3. **The scope fight.** «وين بصير الخلاف عادةً؟» Revisions, "12 posts", ownership, late payment.
4. **Getting paid.** «كيف بتقبض وكم بتتأخر الدفعات؟» Up front, half-half, monthly, chasing.
5. **Their own buying.** «لما تحتاج مصوّر أو ميديا باير، كيف بتلاقيه؟» This is the partner network.
6. **Showing work.** «وين بتعرض شغلك، ومين بيشوفه فعلاً؟» PDF, Instagram, Behance; what they are not allowed to show.
7. **Three messages, ranked.** Show three cards, ask which they would tap and why: (a) «عملاء جدد» find clients, (b) «شركاء تكمّل فريقك» find partners, (c) «نظّم شغلك مع عملاءك الحاليين» simplify client work.
8. **The founding offer.** Read the Founding 100 benefits (docs/39). «أي وحدة بتهمّك فعلاً؟ وأي وحدة ولا بتفرق؟»
9. **The card.** Show the Behind-the-Page card. «بتنشرها؟ ليش لأ؟» Would their clients repost?
10. **Permission.** Anonymous quotes; may we follow up?

Record: kind, size, city, monthly clients, the ranked message, which founding benefits mattered, whether they'd post the card, verbatim quotes.

## Reading the results (decision-focused)

- Buyer segment for the pilot: the segment where ≥ 60% hired in the last 12 months *and* named a hesitation Sawwiq removes (see the work, clear scope, ownership).
- Provider message: the card ranked first by ≥ 50%, judged again after launch by completed profiles and activity, not clicks.
- Kill signals: buyers who found providers only through people they trust *and* had no hesitation (no problem to solve); providers who would not post the card and want only leads.
- Output: one page per audience with counts (raw numbers, not only percentages), five quotes, and the changes to 01 §1 messaging and 05 mechanics.
