# Sawwiq brand kit: voice, dialects, taglines, hashtags, templates

## 1. Brand in one line
**سوّق: أول منصة عربية لوكالات التسويق.** Sawwiq: the first Arabic marketplace for marketing agencies.
**Idea: «سوّق صح»** ("Market right"). Evidence instead of hope: real work, clear prices, contracts in milestones.

## 2. Voice and tone

| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| A trustworthy broker at a clean desk (the deal-desk look) | A hype app, a "guru", a discount bazaar |
| Warm, direct, practical: talk like a smart friend who knows the market | Corporate MSA that sounds translated |
| Specific: numbers, steps, proof | Vague: «الأفضل», «رقم ١», «مضمون ١٠٠٪» ("the best", "number 1", "100% guaranteed") |
| Respectful to agencies: we grow their business | Anti-agency ("agencies will scam you") |
| Honest about limits: "test mode", "Example" | Hiding caveats |

**Rules**
- Arabic is written for Arabs, never translated from English (OneClickConvert rule). Read every line aloud as a Riyadh or Amman business owner would say it.
- One idea per line. Short verbs first: «شوف», «قارن», «ادفع», «اسأل» ("see", "compare", "pay", "ask").
- Numbers: use Western digits (1, 2, 3) in designs, because Readex renders «١» like the letter «ا». Arabic-Indic digits are fine in running captions.
- No absolute claims: never «مضمون» ("guaranteed"), «أرخص» ("cheapest") or «الأفضل» ("the best"). Say «موثّقة» ("verified", meaning registration was checked), «على مراحل» ("in milestones"), «مجاناً خلال الإطلاق» ("free during launch").
- Protected payments: until the licensed provider is live, say «عقد على مراحل، تعتمد كل مرحلة بنفسك» ("a contract in milestones; you approve each one"). The escrow line «المبلغ ينتظر موافقتك» ("the money waits for your approval") is for HOLD assets only (see §8).
- Emojis: only in WhatsApp, TikTok and Snap captions, 1–2 per post (👀 ✅ 🎨 🙏). Never in ads for the health sector.

## 6. Caption templates

**A. Problem → proof → CTA (businesses)**
```
AR: بدّك وكالة تسويق لـ{نوع المصلحة}؟ 👀
على سوّق بتشوف شغلهم الحقيقي وأسعار باقاتهم قبل ما تحكي مع حدا.
جاوب على ٤ أسئلة، والمطابق الذكي يرشّحلك الأنسب في {المدينة}.
🔗 sawwiq.org/match
#سوّق #{وسم_الدولة} #{وسم_القطاع}
EN: Need a marketing agency for your {business}? On Sawwiq, see their real work and package prices before you talk to anyone. Answer 4 questions and the AI matchmaker suggests the best fit in {city}. sawwiq.org/match
```
**B. Agency recruitment**
```
AR: عندك وكالة أو شغل حر في {المدينة}؟ 🎨
اعمل صفحة مجانية على سوّق، اعرض شغلك مجمّع حسب العميل، واستقبل طلبات عملاء حقيقيين.
مجاناً خلال الإطلاق. أول ٤٠ وكالة تحصل على شارة «وكالة مؤسِّسة».
🔗 sawwiq.org/join
EN: Run an agency or freelance in {city}? Create a free Sawwiq page, show work grouped by client and receive real client requests. Free during launch; the first 40 agencies get a Founding badge.
```
**C. Education carousel**
```
AR: قبل ما توقّع مع أي وكالة تسويق، اسأل هالـ٥ أسئلة 👇 احفظ البوست لبعدين.
EN: Before you sign with any marketing agency, ask these 5 questions. Save for later.
```
**D. Agency spotlight** (real agency, consented)
```
AR: وكالة الأسبوع: {اسم الوكالة} من {المدينة} 👏 شغلهم مع {نوع العملاء}… شوف الصفحة كاملة على سوّق.
```
**E. Founder voice (LinkedIn)**
```
AR/EN: 3–5 short paragraphs: an observation from the market → what we built → one number → a question to the audience. No hashtags beyond 2.
```

## 7. Visual system (as built in `marketing/assets/`)
- **Palette:** limestone #F2F2ED, paper #FBFBF8, bottle green #0E6B46 (CTAs, highlights), mint #DCEDE2 (chips), ink #10231A (headlines), muted #4C5C53. Bright and airy; no dark backgrounds.
- **Type:** Readex Pro 600–700 for headlines, IBM Plex Sans Arabic 400–600 for body. Always rendered, never AI-drawn.
- **Logo:** the tile + wordmark from `public/assets/brand/mark.png`. Never redraw, recolour or stretch it. Minimum clear space: ½ tile.
- **Photos:** bright daylight, Amman limestone and Riyadh Najdi architecture, modest wardrobe, invented people (AI-generated, labelled illustrative in the manifest).
- **Product:** only real screen recordings of Sawwiq, with the label «واجهة سوّق الحقيقية · حسابات تجريبية» ("Sawwiq's real interface · demo accounts") while demo agencies are shown.
- **Sonic logo:** `marketing/assets/sting/sawwiq-sonic-logo.*`: whoosh → soft "seal" thud → rising plucked-string and bell chime (~3.5 s). It ends every video (the OneClickConvert "one outro identity" rule).
- **Logo sting:** "Seal" concept: the tile lands like a seal on a deal, the س is written, a glint passes, then the wordmark appears. 9:16 / 16:9 / 1:1 in `assets/sting/`.

## 8. Claim register (check before every ad)

| Claim | Status | Allowed wording |
|---|---|---|
| First Arabic marketplace for agencies | Positioning; **legal sign-off** | «أول منصة عربية لوكالات التسويق» ("the first Arabic marketplace for marketing agencies"). Fallback: «منصة عربية لوكالات التسويق» ("an Arabic marketplace for marketing agencies") |
| Find in a minute | Matcher to results ≈ 10 s measured on the rule-based matcher (25 Sep). **Re-check with the live AI provider.** | «بدقيقة» ("in a minute") |
| Real work | True for real agencies; demo accounts exist | Show «حسابات تجريبية» ("demo accounts") on UI footage until real agencies replace them |
| Verified agencies | Registration checked, not quality | «موثّقة» ("verified"); never «مضمونة» ("guaranteed") |
| Protected / escrow payments | **Test checkout only** | HOLD assets (V09, V12, M3) until a licensed PSP is live and the owner signs off |
| 10% guarantee fee | Code default 10%, but `.env.example` sets 0 at launch | Don't advertise fees; say «مجاناً خلال الإطلاق» ("free during launch") |
| Free for agencies | True while `MONETIZATION_ENABLED=false` | «مجاناً خلال الإطلاق» ("free during launch"), never «مجاناً للأبد» ("free forever") |
| 8 countries | Pages exist; payments are Jordan-only | Don't promise payments outside Jordan |
