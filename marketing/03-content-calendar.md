# Content calendar: first 8 weeks (organic, post by post)

Week 1 starts the Monday after approval. Asset IDs point to `assets/manifest.md`; copy lives in `copy/ad-copy.md` and the caption templates in `02-brand-kit.md` §6.

**Posting times (local):**
- Jordan: 13:00–14:00 and 20:00–22:00.
- KSA: 21:00–23:00.
- LinkedIn: Sunday–Tuesday, 09:00–10:00.

**Channel legend:**
- IG: Instagram (feed, Reel or story)
- TT: TikTok
- SC: Snapchat
- X
- LI: LinkedIn (company page); LI-F: founder's personal profile
- FB: Facebook (mirrors Instagram)
- YT-S: YouTube Shorts
- WA: WhatsApp status/channel

A post marked **(real)** needs real, consented material from a founding agency or client. Never fake it; skip the post if the material doesn't exist yet.

## Always-on (every week)
- **Stories:** 1 story a day on Instagram (poll, Q&A, "agency of the day" repost with consent).
- **Community management:** reply to every comment and DM within 4 working hours; standard replies are in `copy/ad-copy.md` §7.
- **Google Business Profile:** 1 post a week (reuse the week's static).
- **SEO:** 2 articles a week on `/hire/{service}/{city}` topics (`docs/18`), each repurposed as a carousel.
