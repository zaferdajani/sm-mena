# «القائمة» · The List: the pre-launch campaign playbook

> **One line:** Sawwiq is «منصّة المنصّات», the platform of platforms. Every Arab agency, freelancer, creator and influencer who registers before launch gets a permanent, numbered **founding seat**. Low numbers are status, cities race to fill their seats, and every seat holder gets a gold card made to be shared on WhatsApp.

**Live pieces (this branch):**
- `/ar/soon` and `/en/soon`: night-and-gold teaser, the next seat number, the city race and a WhatsApp share button.
- Studio seat card: the provider's seat number, their rank in their city, and a 1080×1920 story image to share on WhatsApp or save.
- The seat is the first ticked step of the studio checklist (endowed progress).
- Visuals made with Higgsfield in `public/teaser/`: the citadel of screens, the invitation card and the golden door. No text, logos or faces.

**Ground rules** (legal and trust):
- Every number is a live database count. Seats are never faked, padded, reissued or sold.
- No "only N seats left" unless a cap is real.
- No launch date until one is certain; slipping dates cost Noon its credibility.
- No real brands, faces or testimonials unless the provider gives written consent.
- The FTC's 2024 rule on fake social-influence indicators and Saudi e-commerce law (up to SAR 1M) both punish fake scarcity.
- **Don't use «ملك الملوك» ("king of kings") in Arabic copy.** A well-known hadith condemns it as a title, and Gulf and Levant audiences would take offence. «منصّة المنصّات» carries the same "above all platforms" idea.

## 1. What the last 10–15 years teach (evidence review, sources in the PR)

| Launch | Mechanic | Result | What we take |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Threads** 2023 | Instagram badge with your **join number** | 100M sign-ups in 5 days, but ~80% of daily users gone in a month | Numbers win the sign-up; a reason to come back wins retention. Our seat is the number; the studio, briefs and launch give the reason. |
| **Monzo** 2016–18 | "Golden Ticket" gifted to one friend to skip the queue | ~40% of 2017 sign-ups, "cost us nothing" | Gifting status is stronger than asking for referrals. → **Founder invitations** (phase 2). |
| **Harry's** 2013 | Referral ladder with real rewards | 100k emails in a week; 77% via referrals | Milestones pull people forward (goal gradient). → City milestones. |
| **Mailbox** 2013 | Live queue: "X ahead of you, Y behind" | 1.3M reservations; bought for ~$100M | Visible rank works. Long waits leak users, so no long gated wait. |
| **Robinhood** 2014 | Queue position + referrals | ~1M waitlist | Rank + social proof. The gamified feel later drew regulators. Keep it honest. |
| **Clubhouse** 2021 | Two invites each; celebrity rooms | Invites resold for up to $400, then −86% | FOMO without a habit collapses. Don't overpromise. |
| **Dribbble** | Players "draft" prospects | Invites became a creative status symbol | The closest analogue to creative professionals: status granted by peers. |
| **Tesla Model 3** 2016 | Ordered reservations | 325k in a week | Commitment and identity ("early adopter of the future"). |
| **Careem** 2012 | Drivers named "Captains"; soft launch in Ramadan | "First Saudi Captainah" became national news | Honorific naming, Ramadan timing and national "firsts" work here. |
| **Anghami** 2012 | MBC / *Arab Idol* partnership | 1M users in about four months | In the Arab world, anchors and broadcast beat viral loops alone. |
| **TikTok Creator Fund** 2020 | $1–2B headline fund | Pennies per 1k views; backlash | Promise founders only what we can deliver. |

## 2. The psychology we use, and how

1. **Ordinal status** (Drèze & Nunes 2009; Threads' number). The seat number, with at least four digits (#0042), feels exclusive while it's low. Show it big and in gold.
2. **Local social proof** (Goldstein, Cialdini & Griskevicius 2008). "Guests in *your room*" beat "guests" as a norm, so we say "رقم 2 في عمّان" ("#2 in Amman"), not just the global number. The city race makes it local.
3. **Basking in reflected glory and local pride** (Cialdini 1976). "Amman vs Irbid", "Riyadh vs Jeddah". Keep rivalry playful and never political.
4. **Scarcity driven by demand** (Worchel 1975). Real sign-ups are the scarcity. "The first seat in Kuwait is still open" is true and pulls.
5. **Endowed progress and goal gradient** (Nunes & Drèze 2006; Kivetz 2006). The seat comes pre-ticked as step 1 of 4, so people finish their pages, which fills the marketplace for launch.
6. **IKEA effect** (Norton, Mochon & Ariely 2012). Founders build their page and portfolio before launch and value it more.
7. **Identity and belonging** (Tajfel & Turner). "شريك مؤسِّس" (founding partner) and a founders' circle on WhatsApp Community.
8. **Reciprocity** (Regan 1971). Give first: a free profile review, a spot in the weekly "new on Sawwiq" carousel (with consent).
9. **Loss aversion and FOMO, used lightly** (Przybylski 2013). "Seats claimed before launch are founding seats. After launch… they're just numbers." It's honest, because founding status really ends at launch. No fake timers.

## 3. Campaign in five acts (dates set by the owner)

| Act | Name | Duration | What happens | Real target |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | **The Seed** «البذرة» | 2 weeks, private | Founder personally invites 30–50 respected providers per launch city (Amman, Riyadh, Jeddah, Dubai, Cairo), and helps them fill their pages. They get seats #0001–#0250. Announce each anchor publicly with their consent. | 150–250 real pages before any public post |
| 1 | **The Door** «الباب» | 1 week | Mystery teasers with no explanation: the golden door and the citadel, captioned «منصّة المنصّات. قريباً.» ("The platform of platforms. Coming soon."). Link in bio → /soon. | Curiosity and follows |
| 2 | **The List** «القائمة» | 2–3 weeks | Seat cards flood WhatsApp Status and Stories. Anchors post theirs first. Every card carries sawwiq.org/soon. | 1,000+ seats |
| 3 | **The Race** «سباق المدن» | 2 weeks | Weekly city leaderboard screenshots and milestone posts ("Irbid passed 50"). Creator partners explain what's coming, with #ad where paid. | 2,500+ seats |
| 4 | **The Crown** «الإطلاق» | Launch week | The client side opens. The first clients see only founders. Founders' badge and founders' night (X/Twitter Space or live event). | Full shelves on day one |

**Timing:** start the race in the weeks before Ramadan. Phone use peaks before iftar and from midnight to 4am, so post then. Careem soft-launched in Ramadan. Keep the tone generous, not hard-sell.

## 4. Ranked tactics (expected impact)
1. **The shareable gold seat card, WhatsApp first.** Done. Next: auto-open it after sign-up.
2. **Anchor recruitment.** 10–30 respected names per market, before anything public.
3. **The city race.** Done on /soon. Next: a weekly screenshot as content, and milestone posts.
4. **Endowed-progress onboarding.** Done (the seat is step 1). Keep adding steps that fill the showcase.
5. **Real founding benefits** (needs owner sign-off):
   - a permanent «مؤسِّس» (founder) badge;
   - early access to client briefs at launch;
   - launch pricing locked when monetization turns on (docs/10).
   Status, not ranking: never boost search order.
6. **Founder invitations.** Each founder can invite 3 peers, and the profile shows "invited by". Monzo's mechanic, fitted to the Arab "علاقات" (relationships) culture. No lotteries or chance-based "golden tickets", to avoid maysir (games of chance).
7. **Ramadan timing** and suhoor-hour posting.
8. **Vision 2030 and creator-economy pride for KSA.** Optional "Mawthooq-licensed" verification for Saudi creators as a genuine badge. Never imply government endorsement.
9. **Creator megaphone.** 20–30 mid-size Arabic creators who teach marketing and freelancing, with honest reviews and #إعلان (#ad) when paid.
10. **Retention before launch.** A founders' WhatsApp Community, a monthly Space, and early looks at features. This is the Threads and Clubhouse lesson.

## 6. Visual system
- Night (#050b08) and gold (#d9ae55 → #f3dca2) over brand green. Arabic geometric borders. No faces.
- Higgsfield prompts that made the current set are in the PR description. Reuse them for new formats: story, square and 16:9.
- The seat card is drawn in the browser, so Arabic shaping is always right; no image-server fonts.

## 7. Measure
- Seats per day by source: put `?utm_source=` on every link (Admin → Statistics).
- Share rate: seat-card shares ÷ new seats.
- Page completion: founders with profile, packages and first post done.
- City coverage: cities with ≥ 1 seat, ≥ 10 seats.

## 8. Owner decisions
1. Founding benefits (badge, early briefs, locked pricing): approve which.
2. Launch date (then a real countdown goes on /soon).
3. Whether /soon replaces the home page during pre-launch.
4. Anchor shortlist, creator shortlist and ad budget.
