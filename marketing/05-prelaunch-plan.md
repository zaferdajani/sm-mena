# Pre-launch plan: make joining Sawwiq the thing to do

> Superseded by the full campaign in `06-the-list-playbook.md` («القائمة», "The List": founding seats, city race, evidence review). This page stays as the short version.

**Goal:** before the public launch, get providers across Jordan, KSA, the Gulf and Egypt to register, and make "are you on Sawwiq yet?" a question people in the industry ask each other. Providers here means agencies, freelancers, content creators, influencers, photographers, designers and media buyers. The landing link is **sawwiq.org/ar/soon** (docs/39).

**Constraints:** nothing gets posted, sent or paid for without owner approval. Numbers are real only. No fake reviews, testimonials, brands or faces. Don't say "free forever". There's no launch date until you pick one.

## 1. The idea: the founding cohort
Scarcity that is true: **the platform is empty, so whoever is first in each city and each service is the first thing clients see.** Every message comes back to one line:

> «شبكة صنّاع السوشيال ميديا العرب. التسجيل مجاني قبل الإطلاق، والأماكن الأولى في مدينتك ما زالت متاحة.»
> ("The network of Arab social media makers. Registration is free before launch, and the first places in your city are still open.")

The live map on `/soon` does the FOMO work. "Riyadh: 14 · Kuwait: first place open" makes a Kuwaiti agency want to be that first tile. It is honest at any number.

## 3. Levers that make it a trend
1. **Founding member badge** (needs your sign-off, then I build it). A "عضو مؤسس · Founding member" mark on the profiles of everyone registered before launch day. It is status, not ranking: it never changes search order (CLAUDE.md, relevance rule). This is the strongest reason to join before launch.
2. **Invite a colleague.** Personal share links from `/soon` and the studio ("I'm on Sawwiq, join me"). Later: a public "invited by" count on the profile. No cash rewards before payments exist.
3. **Shareable "I'm in" card.** After registering, a ready-made 1080×1920 story: the provider's logo plus «أنا من أوائل سوّق» ("I'm one of Sawwiq's first") plus their city. Every share is free reach into exactly the right audience. I can build this next.
4. **The live map as content.** Post a screenshot every week. Cities compete, local pride does the rest.
5. **Creators as the megaphone.** Invite 20–30 mid-size Arabic creators who teach marketing or freelancing (10k–200k followers) to register and review the platform honestly. Any paid post is labelled as an ad, and there are no scripted "testimonials".
6. **Communities.** Freelancer Facebook groups and WhatsApp communities in Jordan, Saudi and Egypt, university design and media faculties, co-working spaces (Amman, Riyadh, Cairo), and industry events. Share through admins, with their permission.
7. **Short, repeatable content.** «شو بصير لما عميل يدوّر على مصمم في الرياض؟» ("What happens when a client looks for a designer in Riyadh?") The answer: they'll find whoever is on Sawwiq. Use the existing reels and sting in `marketing/assets`.

## 4. Channels and budget (suggested; owner approves spend)
- **Organic first:** Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn (agency owners), X (Saudi industry), Snapchat (KSA).
- **Paid:** Meta, with lead-to-/soon ads aimed at people interested in freelancing, marketing and content creation in JO, SA, AE and EG. Start at about 10–15 JOD a day per country for 2 weeks, then move money to whichever city signs up cheapest. LinkedIn: small agency-owner audience only.
- **Measure:** registrations per day by source (`?utm_source=` on every link; Admin → Statistics), pages completed (not just accounts), shares from `/soon`.

## 5. Ready-to-use lines (AR first)
- «قبل ما يوصل العملاء… كن أنت الموجود.» "Before the clients arrive, be the one who's already there."
- «كل مدينة لها أول وكالة على سوّق. ليش ما تكون أنت؟» "Every city will have its first agency on Sawwiq. Why not you?"
- «مجاني الآن. دقيقتين. وصفحتك جاهزة يوم الإطلاق.» "Free for now. Two minutes. Your page ready on launch day."
- «مصمم؟ مصور؟ صانع محتوى؟ مكانك محجوز… لو سجّلت.» "Designer? Photographer? Creator? Your spot is held… if you register."
