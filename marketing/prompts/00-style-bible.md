# Sawwiq visual style bible (applies to every prompt)

Owner direction: bright, cheerful, light. Never dark or moody.

## Look
- Daylight, high-key, warm-neutral white balance. Soft window light, gentle shadows.
- Palette on set: pale limestone (#F2F2ED), paper white (#FBFBF8), bottle green (#0E6B46) accents, mint (#DCEDE2), ink green (#10231A) only in small details. Plants welcome.
- Places: real Levant and Gulf settings. Amman limestone buildings, Riyadh modern Najdi-inspired architecture. No clichés (no camels, no desert sunsets, no souq smoke).
- Lens: 35–50 mm feel, shallow depth, clean backgrounds, commercial documentary.

## People (modest, respectful, credible)
- Adults 25–55, dressed like real business owners. Women: hijab or modest styling, long sleeves, chest covered. Men: shirts, thobe and shemagh in Saudi scenes.
- No touching between unrelated men and women. Handshakes only man–man, or hands-only shots.
- Expressions: calm confidence and relief. No exaggerated "stock" grins.
- Invented people only. No lookalikes of real people or influencers.

## Product and text rules (from the OneClickConvert ad-director rules)
- Never let a model draw UI, logos or Arabic text. Screens stay blank white or turned away and get composited in post with real screenshots of sawwiq.org.
- All Arabic type is rendered in Chromium (Readex Pro for headings, IBM Plex Sans Arabic for body) and overlaid in post.
- No brand logos, no readable signs, no watermarks.
- Short takes: one beat per clip (4–5 s), assembled in post. Each clip is regenerated on its own if rejected.
- Audio: ambient only ("no voices, no speech, no music"). VO, sonic logo and bed are added in post.

## Negative prompt block (append to every prompt)
no readable text, no letters, no logos, no watermark, no subtitles, no extra fingers, no distorted hands, no dark or gloomy lighting, no revealing clothing
